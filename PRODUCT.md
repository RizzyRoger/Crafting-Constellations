# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React + TypeScript, Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Storage, Hosting). Developed against the Firebase emulators; see README.md. The static sample boards in `samples/` remain the visual reference.

## Users

Primary users are people whose families have cancer. They are not necessarily the patient. They want to reach outward — to friends, community, and people who care but do not know how to help — rather than only to other families on the same path.

Assumption: the typical user is a family member or caregiver under emotional and logistical load, looking for a quieter way to ask, receive, and stay connected.

## Scope (from the spec, 2026-10-10)

The spec PDF ("Crafting Constellations", partly outdated — its compute/AI template sections are ignored) defines the first build:

- Pen-name accounts (email/password or Google); a verified email unlocks writing in groups, private messages and reaching out.
- Constellation page: groups as clusters, people as stars, friendships as lines; a list view says the same in text.
- Groups with talk, posts, comments and likes; private one-to-one notes between connected people.
- Licensed mental health professionals apply with documents; moderators approve; a circled "licensed" mark appears.
- AutoMod holds harmful writing before anyone sees it; reports; a moderator review queue; bans.
- Primary users widen from "families with cancer" to families living alongside any illness, plus licensed professionals who want to help.

## Product Purpose

Crafting Constellations is a therapeutic social networking app. It exists so families living with cancer can outreach: make needs visible, let people who want to help actually help, and stay in relationship without performing wellness or managing a public feed.

Success is that outreach feels possible and dignified — someone can ask, someone can show up, and the family does not have to carry the social labor alone.

## Positioning

A therapeutic network for family outreach during cancer, not a general social feed and not a medical portal. The mechanism is helping a family reach the people around them who already want to help, and giving those people a place to act.

## Operating Context

Used in the middle of real life: hospital waits, late nights, group texts that have gone quiet, people asking "what can I do?" The first deliverable is a design board that establishes the visual world for that product, not the application itself.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed for this phase:

- The requested surface is a design board for the product, not a working social app.
- Visual work must be based on the provided watercolor gray swatch sheet (hand-washed pigment, paper, handwritten pigment notes).
- Stack is plain static HTML/CSS.
- Locked visual world: The Value Study. Recorded as a reference only; the user paused before any HTML/CSS build.

Undecided:

- App feature set beyond outreach (feeds, messaging, care calendars, fundraising, clinical content).
- Native vs web for the eventual product.
- Whether patients themselves are a first-class audience.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Crafting Constellations.
- Binding visual reference: the watercolor gray swatch sheet (titanium, graphite, Payne's, fresco, caliente, and Joseph Z's neutral / cool / warm grays). Handmade washes, granulation, paper white, handwritten annotation.
- Locked visual world (2026-10-10): The Value Study — the product's world is a watercolor value study. Pigment, paper, and handwritten notes lead; unpainted paper is a designed cell; one wash owns the first viewport; the opening stroke enters on a brush path. Reference comp: `.impeccable/references/value-study.webp`. Source swatch: `.impeccable/references/swatch-sheet.jpg`. Not a build approval.
- Voice implied by the brief: therapeutic, not clinical; social, not performative. Do not invent a tagline that claims medical outcomes.

## Evidence on Hand

- Watercolor swatch photograph provided by the user (nine gray washes with pigment codes and staining/transparency notes).
- No testimonials, case studies, screenshots of an existing product, or real user stories. Future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

- Dignity over engagement — never optimize for posting, streaks, or performance.
- Outreach is the job — make it easier for a family to be reached, and for people who want to help to act.
- Quiet material honesty — the product should feel handmade and human, not clinical or like a wellness brand.
- Do not invent proof — no fake families, quotes, outcomes, or medical claims.
- Load-aware — assume the reader may be exhausted; keep language and structure gentle and clear.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Users may be depleted, interrupted, or cognitively overloaded. Future product UI should favor high legibility, low visual noise, and language that does not assume medical literacy. Cancer experience includes many family structures, ages, and cultures; do not default to a single household type.
